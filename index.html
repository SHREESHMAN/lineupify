WHY LINEUPIFY

Music discovery, with the controls left to you.

The server keeps generation inspectable: inputs become artist seeds, candidates are ranked and matched, filters are applied, and the result stays a draft until you choose to publish.

Lineups & seeds

Start with a festival poster, lineup text, mood, genre, artist, song, chart, country, playlist or taste profile.

Exact matching

Rank candidate songs from available music data, then match them to Spotify by ISRC with text search as fallback.

Editable drafts

Inspect tracks and unresolved artists, swap or remove items, reorder, undo, then publish only when the draft is right.

Local by design

Runs on your machine with your own Spotify app. There is no Lineupify server and no telemetry.

HOW IT WORKS

From a rough idea to a finished set.

01

Give it a seed

Describe what you want, attach a lineup, name an artist or point to a playlist.

02

Build the artist list

Lineupify expands seeds and resolves artists using Deezer, Last.fm and Spotify where appropriate.

03

Shape the draft

Rank tracks, match them, dedupe, filter, order and checkpoint the draft to disk.

04

Review & publish

Edit what you want, then publish to Spotify or export the draft as CSV, M3U, links, text or Markdown.
